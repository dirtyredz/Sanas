# Keep the mic-only user identity across reconnects and resume


In mic-only (Elsewhere) meetings, "Me" depends on the user's "that's me" pin of a diarized index, and Deepgram renumbers speakers across reconnects and resume. Turn grouping, the classifier's candidate speaker and Next point coaching all depend on that identity; after a renumbering the user is unknown or wrong until re-pinned. Investigate carrying the pin across connections (voice heuristic, overlap with the last known pinned speech) or prompting to re-pin. Follow-up to dk-f004d957.

Implemented 2026-11-09: explicit reconfirmation, not voice matching. Mono speaker IDs are allocated per meeting and mapped independently per connection; resets precede replay transcripts and resume creates a fresh mapping. Historical pins/rows/names remain isolated. After a prior pin, new speech is uncertain and automatic answers/coaching are suspended until a current voice is pinned; manual Answer now remains available. Historical corrections do not confirm the current connection. Main validates membership and publishes current/selected IDs; Live and overlay persist the re-pin cue, await pin failures, and relabel shared transcript state from confirmed selection. Stereo identity and batch time-overlap carryover are unchanged. Covered by provider/meeting fake sockets, assistance cancellation, SQLite repository, renderer, and preview tests.
