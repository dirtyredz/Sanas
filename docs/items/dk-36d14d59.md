# Live presentation controls: per-meeting context, typed questions, More detail, Ask the audience


From the first real presentation (Constellation, 2026-10-30): the deck changed on the fly, the job-level deck/notes could not follow, and the owner had no way to type into Sanas mid-meeting (fell back to typing questions to Claude Code).

Acceptance:
- Per-meeting context: during a live meeting the owner can add/edit free-text context (and replace the slide list) that applies immediately to that meeting's answers and coaching, without stopping the meeting. Job context stays the default; meeting context is saved with the meeting.
- Typed input in the overlay: type a question or instruction and get an answer from the same answer path (whole job + meeting context + transcript). Keyboard-first; does not steal focus from the presentation app until clicked.
- 'More detail' button on an answer card: expands that answer with deeper specifics (same grounding), shown in place or as a follow-up card.
- 'Ask the audience' button: suggests one good question to put to the audience based on what was just covered and the context.
- Meetings that use none of this behave as today.
